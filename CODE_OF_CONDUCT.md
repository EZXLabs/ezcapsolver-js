# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on ideas, not individuals.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of community discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Report concerns to: admin@ezxlabs.com
